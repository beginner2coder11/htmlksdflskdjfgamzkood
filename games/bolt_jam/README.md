# Bolt Jam: screw-sorting puzzle template

Coloured screws pin glossy acrylic plates to a pegboard. Tap a screw to unscrew it: it spins out and flies
into the toolbox of its colour. A toolbox holds 3 screws, then its lid snaps shut and the next box slides in.
Screws with no matching open box wait in the spare holes, and if every spare hole is full and no free screw
fits an open box, you're out of space. A plate left on one screw swings down on it, and a plate with no
screws drops off the board. Everything (plates, screws, boxes, sound) is made in code, with no build step,
no dependencies and no image or sound files. It plays fully offline.

## Controls
Tap a screw. Screws under another plate are stuck: free the plates on top first (a stuck screw shakes and
the plate covering it flashes). P or Esc pauses.

## Features
- **120 levels** (every 10th is a HARD level with more plates and double coins), rebuilt from a seed, so no
  level data ships and a new seed gives a whole new set.
- **Always solvable:** each level is generated by stacking plates and playing out a legal unscrew order.
  A smart-player simulation then rearranges the colours so it has to park more screws in spare holes,
  while it still clears the level within the level's spare-hole budget. Its winning order is stored with the
  level, and the test below replays it through the real game for all 120 levels.
- **Physics:** a plate on its last screw swings as a damped pendulum (its weight and shape matter) and
  settles hanging under the screw. A plate with no screws left drops off with a spin. Which screws are
  covered is worked out from the plates' real shapes and current poses.
- **Look:** translucent glossy plates with thickness, soft shadows and drilled holes; metallic screw heads
  with washers and 5 head styles (Phillips, slotted, hex, star, gem); a threaded shank shows as a screw lifts
  out; toolboxes with handles, labels and a snapping lid with a check badge; a steel spare-hole rail that
  pulses red when it's nearly full; sparks, confetti and combo words (NICE!, GREAT!, AWESOME!).
- **6 boards** (Workshop, Mint, Sky, Berry, Night, Oak) and 5 screw-head styles in the shop.
- **Boosters:** Extra Hole (one more spare hole) and Drill (pull out any screw, even a covered one).
- **Ads and retention:** a rewarded "+1 spare hole" to continue when you run out of space, double coins on a
  win, free boosters for an ad, an interstitial every 3 wins (`js/ads.js` uses the `GameAdsBridge`
  contract), and a 7-day daily gift.
- **Sound (js/audio.js):** ratchet clicks as a screw turns out, a wooden "thock" that climbs in pitch as a box
  fills, metal clinks for spare holes, a clunk and chime when a box closes, a whoosh and clatter when a plate
  drops, and a soft marimba loop. Sound effects and music have separate toggles.

## Difficulty audit
Results across the 120 levels (built-in bot, which also plays behind the title screen):
- The stored solution of every level clears in the real game: 120 / 120.
- A smart player (fits a box when it can, otherwise parks the colour that comes up soonest) needs about
  1 spare hole on early levels and 3–4 on late ones, and clears 110 / 120 on its own.
- A sloppy player (half of the taps on a random free screw) clears about 93% of levels 1–30 and about 60% of
  levels 91–120.

```js
UI.play(n);                       // or Game.start(n)
for (const [plate, hole] of Game.level.order) { /* wait until boxes settle, then */
  Game.take(Game.screws.find(s => s.plate === plate && s.hole === hole)); }
```

## Reskin
- **Name, logo and UI colours:** `title`, `titleLines` (one entry per logo line) and `theme` (the CSS variables
  from `css/style.css`) in `js/config.js`. `js/skin.js` applies them when the game starts.
- `js/config.js` also holds the screw colours, plate tints and transparency, the level count, seed and
  difficulty curve (plates, colours, mixing), box size and how many boxes are open, spare holes, physics,
  booster prices, coins, the boards and screw heads in the shop, the daily gift, ads and the storage
  prefix (`bj_`).
- `js/levels.js` holds the plate shapes (bar, rectangle, circle, triangle, hexagon) and the generator.
- `js/game.js` draws everything: `bakePlate`, `bakeHead`, `bakeBoard` and `drawBox`.
